# Commission Quote

The home page at `/` generates a commission quote from loan details. No sign-in is required.

The form has Loan amount and Loan term (months) inputs, a Risk band dropdown with Low, Medium and High options, and a Generate Quote button. All fields are required.

A successful submission displays Your commission quote, Total commission,
Commission rate and Quote ID. The quote ID is generated by the mock vendor and
must be nonblank; its exact value changes for each quote.
