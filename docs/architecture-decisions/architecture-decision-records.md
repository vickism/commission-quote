# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

## What are ADRs?

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) are a way to document important decisions regarding the architecture of a system. They capture the context, reasons, and outcomes of key architectural decisions, providing a historical record and rationale that can be consulted and referenced by current and future team members.

For more information, please visit the [ADRs homepage](https://adr.github.io/).

## Why are ADRs Important?

ADRs are crucial for several reasons:

- **Documentation**: They provide a structured and consistent way to document architectural decisions.
- **Communication**: They facilitate clear communication among team members and stakeholders about why certain decisions were made.
- **Historical Record**: They serve as a historical record that can be referenced in the future, helping to understand the evolution of the system's architecture.
- **Guidance**: They help ensure that decisions are made consistently and in line with the project's goals and principles.

## Guiding Principles

Our architectural decisions are guided by a set of principles to ensure that the system is robust, secure, and scalable while providing a seamless user experience. These principles are detailed in our [Principles Document](../architecture-principles.md).

Please ensure that each ADR aligns with these principles and provides clear and comprehensive documentation of the decisions made.
